About me

From scaling enterprise platforms to shipping AI products

I started out delivering large digital programs. I helped stand up a platform that coordinated each CAR-T patient's journey from apheresis to infusion, and I turned 500-item backlogs into releases teams could ship. Along the way I watched the industry shift, first to digital patient services and then to generative AI. Each time, I moved toward the change early instead of waiting for it to reach me.

Today I take AI products from discovery to working prototype to funded, adopted product, for R&D, Commercial and Medical Affairs teams. I often have a working prototype before the business case is written, and I'm at ease with the ambiguity and cross-functional tension that come with that.

I know how regulated this industry is. That doesn't deter me. It makes me double down on being the disruptor, because the ideas that make it through those constraints are the ones that matter. Outside work I keep building: a hackathon-winning clinical concept, a household finance app and a daily newsletter, all coded with AI tools.

Hackathon winner · Clinical AI

Vital Thread

An AI handoff companion for post-anesthesia (PACU) nurses. It won the Women in Product hackathon.

Voice AIClinical workflow
Read the case study
The problem
When a patient leaves the PACU, the incoming nurse gets the chart but loses the human context: family dynamics, anxiety, what comforts the patient. EHRs aren't built to hold it.
The idea
Nurses capture that context by voice, and Vital Thread structures it into an SBAR card for the next nurse. It's a mobile-first layer alongside the EHR, with a QR-code handoff.
What came next
After the win, we ran initial market research on the competitive landscape and the PACU nursing workflow to test whether the gap was real.
Personal build · Fintech

Home Ledger

A shared finance app for couples who want one picture of their money without giving up privacy.

Next.jsSupabasePlaid
Read the case study
The problem
Apps for couples tend to do one job: net worth, splitting expenses or goals. Partners want shared visibility, but not a feed of every purchase.
What I built
Net worth from linked bank accounts, one netted settle-up and goals funded in a waterfall. Balances are shared, while each partner's transactions stay private until marked shared.
How I ran it
Specs, wireframes and user flows first. One AI agent builds and a separate one audits, so code is never tested by the agent that wrote it. I cut recurring bills from the MVP to protect scope.
